# Hypercube benchmark (hypercube-prompt-v2)

## The puzzle

An n-dimensional 3x3x...x3 twisty puzzle. n = 3 is the ordinary Rubik's cube;
n = 4 is the 3^4 hypercube (as in Magic Cube 4D); n = 5 and 6 go further.
Axes are named x, y, z, w, v, u; an n-dimensional problem uses the first n.

- Pieces sit at integer positions p in {-1,0,1}^n, written (x,y,z,...).
- A piece has one sticker for each nonzero coordinate: if p_a = +1 it has a
  sticker facing +a, if p_a = -1 one facing -a. A sticker's location is
  (position, facing).
- Colours are named after directions (+x, -w, ...). On the solved puzzle every
  sticker's colour equals the direction it faces.

## Moves

A move is written S:i>j, where S is a signed axis (for example +w) and i, j
are two different axes, both different from S's axis.

- It acts on the layer of pieces whose S-axis coordinate equals the sign of S
  (+w:... acts on every piece with w = +1).
- Those pieces rotate 90 degrees in the i-j plane so that the +i direction
  turns into +j: new_j = old_i, new_i = -old_j, all other coordinates unchanged.
- Each sticker's facing turns the same way: +i -> +j, +j -> -i, -i -> -j,
  -j -> +i; facings along other axes are unchanged.
- S:j>i is the inverse of S:i>j. Pieces outside the layer do not move.

Example (n = 3): +z:x>y moves the piece at (1,0,1) to (0,1,1); its sticker
facing +x now faces +y, and its sticker facing +z still faces +z.

## Problems

### track-000
n = 4. Moves, applied in order: +w:y>z, -y:w>x, +x:z>y, +w:x>y, +y:x>z, -w:z>y, -x:z>y, -y:z>w, -w:x>y, -x:y>w, +y:w>x, +w:y>x, -y:w>z, +y:x>w, +z:y>x, -z:y>x, -x:w>z, -w:x>z, -x:y>w, -z:x>y, +x:y>z, +y:x>w, +x:z>y, -z:x>y
Start: the sticker at (1,0,1,1) facing +x.
Question: after all moves, at which position and facing is that sticker?

### track-001
n = 4. Moves, applied in order: -x:y>z, +y:x>w, -x:z>y, -z:y>x, -w:x>y, -z:w>x, +w:x>z, -z:x>y, +y:x>w, -z:w>y, -x:w>z, +x:z>w, -x:z>y, -y:w>z, +z:y>x, -x:y>z, -y:z>w, -x:y>w, -y:w>x, -x:z>w, -w:x>z, -z:x>y, -y:z>x, -z:y>w
Start: the sticker at (-1,0,-1,1) facing -z.
Question: after all moves, at which position and facing is that sticker?

### track-002
n = 6. Moves, applied in order: +y:x>u, -x:z>u, +z:w>u, -y:u>w, +y:w>u, +v:x>z, +x:v>u, -x:v>u, +x:v>u, +y:x>w, -w:v>y, +u:w>z, +x:z>v, -v:x>y, -y:z>w, -z:v>u, -v:y>x, -u:w>z, +w:x>z, -v:w>u, +w:z>u, -w:v>u, -v:u>x, +w:x>v
Start: the sticker at (0,1,1,0,1,0) facing +v.
Question: after all moves, at which position and facing is that sticker?

### track-003
n = 4. Moves, applied in order: +x:y>z, -z:y>x, -w:z>x, +x:y>w, +y:x>z, +z:y>w, -z:w>x, +y:w>x, +z:w>x, +y:x>w, +x:z>w, +w:y>z

Question: after all moves, at which position and facing is that sticker?

### track-004
n = 5. Moves, applied in order: -y:w>x, -z:w>x, -x:y>w, +v:z>y, -x:v>y, +y:x>w, +v:z>y, -w:z>y, +v:w>x, -v:z>w, -x:z>w, -y:z>x, -x:v>w, +x:y>v, +z:x>w, +w:z>y, -v:w>x, +z:v>y, -v:z>w, -y:v>w, -v:y>z, +x:y>v, -z:x>y, -x:w>y

Question: after all moves, at which position and facing is that sticker?

### track-005
n = 6. Moves, applied in order: +w:u>y, -x:w>u, +u:x>z, +x:v>u, -v:u>x, -z:x>y, -u:x>y, -v:y>x, -u:w>v, -x:w>u, +w:v>z, +v:u>x
Start: the sticker at (-1,1,-1,1,0,-1) facing -u.
Question: after all moves, at which position and facing is that sticker?

## Answer format

Work everything out yourself. End your reply with exactly one line per problem, in order:
<id>: (<coordinates>) <facing>        e.g.  track-000: (1,0,-1,1) +z
